Help

Inspect locally

Paste a compact signed JWT. Header and payload are decoded as UTF-8 JSON, without sending the token anywhere. Encrypted tokens (JWE) are not supported.

Verify a signature

Choose the HMAC algorithm you expect, enter a key as UTF-8, hex, Base64 or Base64url, then press Verify. The selection must match the header. HS256, HS384 and HS512 are supported; other algorithms can be inspected but not verified. Critical header extensions are rejected.

Claims

exp, iat and nbf are Unix seconds, shown in UTC with relative times. A matching signature does not establish audience, issuer, authorization or claim validity. Dev use only.

Tokens and keys are held only in memory. Web Crypto requires HTTPS or localhost.
